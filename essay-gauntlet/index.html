Essay Gauntlet

A rubric screen, then four readers: a classmate, your teacher or professor, a more experienced writer and a renowned expert on your topic. Every flag comes back to you to fix.

Reviews are written by Claude playing each role. The rubric desk simulates how a grader typically checks an essay against its assignment; real teachers, editors and rubrics differ. Your essay is sent to Claude only when a review runs; nothing is saved on a server, and your results stay in this browser. If this is for a class, check your school's policy on AI help before you submit a revised version.
