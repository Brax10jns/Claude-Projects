Resume Gauntlet

An ATS screen, then four reviewers: an interviewer, a rival applicant, a field expert and an executive. Every flag comes back to you to fix.

Reviews are written by Claude playing each role. The ATS desk simulates how applicant tracking systems and recruiter filters typically score a resume; real systems differ by employer. Your resume is sent to Claude only when a review runs; nothing is saved on a server, and your results stay in this browser.
