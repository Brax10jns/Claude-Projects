How the scores work
Life Score
Weighted average of the pillar scores for the day: . A pillar with no data that day is left out and the other weights are scaled up. DEMO pillars are never counted.
Sleep
75% hours asleep vs your goal, 25% restorative sleep (deep + REM) vs a 40% target. A night counts toward the day you wake up.
Recovery
Today's HRV (65%) and resting heart rate (35%) against your own previous 30 days. 50 means a normal day for you. It needs 7 days of history first ("calibrating").
Training
70% Apple Watch exercise minutes vs goal, 30% steps vs goal.
Work
60% focused hours (RescueTime "productive" and "very productive") vs goal, 40% productivity pulse. Days with less than the minimum tracked time show no data.
Tasks
Share of that day's events in your Tasks calendar whose title has ✓ or [x]. No tasks planned = no data.
Bands
▲ Strong 67–100 · ● Steady 34–66 · ▼ Low 0–33.
